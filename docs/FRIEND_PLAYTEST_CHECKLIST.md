# Friend Playtest Checklist

For two players on one keyboard, after the overhaul. Fill in one copy per session. Use a save
slot you do not need: if either knight dies, the run restarts at level 1.

**Controls:** Player One W A S D move, **V** attack. Player Two arrows move, **Alt** attack.

- Tap attack to slash.
- Keep attack held after a slash until the knight glints, then release for a **heavy
  slash**. It breaks shields and cracked blocks.
- Attack downward in the air to **bounce** off enemies and lanterns.
- Hitting the same enemy within a moment of your partner is a **co-op strike** (extra damage).
- Jumping off your partner's head goes much higher.
